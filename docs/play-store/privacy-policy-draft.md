# Lakebound privacy policy — draft, not yet published

Applies to Lakebound 0.1.0, package com.zylaah.lakebound.
Date: 19 October 2026.

Lakebound is an offline lake-exploration prototype. This version does not require an account and does not collect or transmit personal information through the game. It includes no advertising, analytics, in-app purchases, chat or online services.

The current game has no user profile or cloud save. It does not request access to location, contacts, microphone, camera or shared device storage. Godot may write local diagnostic files on the device; this version does not send those files to the developer. Local application data can be removed by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app.

Google Play and the operating system may process installation, diagnostic or account information under their own privacy policies, independently of this game.

If you contact the developer by email, the developer will receive information you choose to include and will use it to respond to your request. The developer must supply a contact address and confirm their handling/retention practices before publishing this policy.
