# Remote player motion (334 ms policy)

RemoteAngler renders a 180 ms snapshot buffer with a maximum 450 ms prediction beyond the latest receipt. Reported velocity handles strafing; reported speed drives animation independently from visual correction. A 120 ms exponential correction blends packet arrival discrepancies. Teleports over thirty units and reconnections reset playback; stale avatars hide after ten seconds. Buffers hold at most sixteen samples. Identical-time burst samples coalesce. Existing transport sequence checks reject out-of-order/duplicate packets.

MatchService.pose_interval owns the numeric policy with a local minimum 334 ms. Session checks movement, turning, fishing state AND speed changes, including stops at an unchanged position. It also sends regularly while moving and every six seconds when stationary. A successful service send on a joined Realtime channel is required before clearing dirty tracking; connecting sockets preserve pending poses. All sends use the central wall-clock limiter; there are no extra start/stop bypass packets.

Migration 014 installs one-room/five-player admission, 334 ms new-room policy, and 90 events/sec reservation for duration+180 seconds, retaining the rolling 33-date budget and all previous reservations. Conservative full-room fanout is 25/0.334 = 74.85 events/sec, plus control headroom. The app's limiter/admission do not protect against arbitrary modified clients or usage by unrelated services. Provider plan limits must still be monitored.

Existing nonleased legacy rooms keep their previously reserved two-second policy until rematch. At migration time, at most one currently leased room can be upgraded after topping up the reservation by (duration+180)*75. Migration refuses multiple leased rooms or insufficient budget atomically. Rematch reserves a complete new round at the new rate before changing room policy. Older game binaries reject the faster policy; restart both clients after migration, then create a new room or rematch.

Opt-in debug: LAKEBOUND_MOTION_DEBUG=1 prints MOTION_TX interval/gap/speed every three seconds (at most 100 lines) and MOTION_RX arrival-gap/correction/speed/reset every second (at most 300 lines per peer). It never prints credentials, player names or packet bodies. Normal sessions do not print motion traces.
